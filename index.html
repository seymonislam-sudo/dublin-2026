Airport to hotel

Dublin Express or Aircoach from outside arrivals into the city centre, about 30 minutes. For four of us with bags, one taxi is just as easy.

Getting around

Walk. For taxis, use the FREENOW app. It's how Dublin books cabs.

Know before you go

Euros, but cards work everywhere. Same plugs as the UK, so no adapters. Bring photo ID for the flight; check what Aer Lingus accepts. Pack a waterproof.

Emergencies

112 or 999, both work in Ireland.

House Rules

Binding on all six. Breaches are punishable by buying the round.

  1. Nobody says "top o' the morning". Not ironically, not at all.
  2. Louise and Helen are the designated grown-ups. When they say it's the last one, it's the last one. (Jeff and Seymon may appeal once.)
  3. You may not dance with your own partner for the whole lesson. Swap. Suffer together.
  4. Every round is a round for six. No "I'm just getting one for us two".
  5. Lost your other half? Check the nearest pub with a fiddle in it.
  6. What happens on the Viking boat is shouted, loudly, at the rest of Dublin.
